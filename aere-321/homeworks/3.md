# AERE 321 Homework 3

## 1.

![](https://i.imgur.com/pGJJfl2m.png)

Given:

$$
P = 150kN
$$

$$
L = 1m
$$

$$
h = 0.3m
$$

$$
b = 0.1m
$$

$$
E = 68.9GPa
$$

$$
G = 26GPa
$$

Energies:

$$
U_e = \frac{1}{2} P \Delta
$$

![](https://i.imgur.com/Tol1XJj.png)

$$
0 = V + P \implies V = -P
$$

$$
0 = -M - Px \implies M = -Px
$$

$$
A = bh = 0.3m * 0.1m = 0.03m^2
$$

$$
f_s = \frac{6}{5}
$$

$$
I = \frac{1}{12} b h^3 = \frac{1}{12} 0.1m * (0.3m)^3 = 2.25 * 10^{-4} m^4
$$

$$
U_i = \int_0^L \frac{f_s V^2 dx}{2 G A} + \int_0^L \frac{M^2 dx}{2 E I}
$$

$$
U_i = \frac{6}{5} \int_0^L \frac{P^2 dx}{2 G A} + \int_0^L \frac{P^2 x^2 dx}{2 E I}
$$

$$
U_i = \frac{3}{5} \frac{P^2}{G A} \int_0^L dx + \frac{P^2}{2 E I} \int_0^L x^2 dx
$$

$$
U_i = \frac{3}{5} \frac{P^2 L}{G A} + \frac{1}{6} \frac{P^2 L^3}{E I}
$$

$$
U_e = U_i
$$

$$
\frac{1}{2} P \Delta = \frac{3}{5} \frac{P^2 L}{G A} + \frac{1}{6} \frac{P^2 L^3}{E I}
$$

$$
\Delta = \frac{6}{5} \frac{P L}{G A} + \frac{1}{3} \frac{P L^3}{E I}
$$

$$
\Delta = \frac{6}{5} \frac{150kN * 1m}{26GPa * 0.03m^2} + \frac{1}{3} \frac{150kN * (1m)^3}{68.9GPa * 2.25 * 10^{-4} m^4} = \boxed{3.46mm}
$$
