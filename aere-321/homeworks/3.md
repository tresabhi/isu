## 1.

![](https://i.imgur.com/pGJJfl2m.png)

Given:

$$
P = 150kN
$$

$$
L = 1m
$$

$$
h = 0.3m
$$

$$
b = 0.1m
$$

$$
E = 68.9GPa
$$

$$
G = 26GPa
$$

Energies:

$$
U_e = \frac{1}{2} P \Delta
$$

![](https://i.imgur.com/Tol1XJj.png)

$$
0 = V + P \implies V = -P
$$

$$
0 = -M - Px \implies M = -Px
$$

$$
A = bh = 0.3m * 0.1m = 0.03m^2
$$

$$
f_s = \frac{6}{5}
$$

$$
I = \frac{1}{12} b h^3 = \frac{1}{12} 0.1m * (0.3m)^3 = 2.25 * 10^{-4} m^4
$$

$$
U_i = \int_0^L \frac{f_s V^2 dx}{2 G A} + \int_0^L \frac{M^2 dx}{2 E I}
$$

$$
U_i = \frac{6}{5} \int_0^L \frac{P^2 dx}{2 G A} + \int_0^L \frac{P^2 x^2 dx}{2 E I}
$$

$$
U_i = \frac{3}{5} \frac{P^2}{G A} \int_0^L dx + \frac{P^2}{2 E I} \int_0^L x^2 dx
$$

$$
U_i = \frac{3}{5} \frac{P^2 L}{G A} + \frac{1}{6} \frac{P^2 L^3}{E I}
$$

$$
U_e = U_i
$$

$$
\frac{1}{2} P \Delta = \frac{3}{5} \frac{P^2 L}{G A} + \frac{1}{6} \frac{P^2 L^3}{E I}
$$

$$
\Delta = \frac{6}{5} \frac{P L}{G A} + \frac{1}{3} \frac{P L^3}{E I}
$$

$$
\Delta = \frac{6}{5} \frac{150kN * 1m}{26GPa * 0.03m^2} + \frac{1}{3} \frac{150kN * (1m)^3}{68.9GPa * 2.25 * 10^{-4} m^4} = \boxed{3.46mm}
$$

## 2.

![](https://i.imgur.com/llWyi3sm.png)

Given:

$$
E = 70GPa
$$

$$
m = 10kg
$$

$$
h = 150mm
$$

Preliminaries:

$$
L_{AB} = 60mm
$$

$$
L_{BC} = 120mm
$$

$$
A_{AB} = \frac{\pi}{4} d_{AB}^2 = \frac{\pi}{4} (20mm)^2 = 314.2mm^2
$$

$$
A_{BC} = \frac{\pi}{4} d_{BC}^2 = \frac{\pi}{4} (40mm)^2 = 1257mm^2
$$

$$
k_{AB} = \frac{A_{AB} E}{L_{AB}} = \frac{314.2mm^2 * 70GPa}{60mm} = 366.57kN/mm
$$

$$
k_{BC} = \frac{A_{BC} E}{L_{BC}} = \frac{1257mm^2 * 70GPa}{120mm} = 733.3kN/mm
$$

Energies:

$$
W = mg = 10kg * 9.81m/s^2 = 98.1N
$$

$$
U_e = W (h + \Delta)
$$

$$
U_{AB} = \frac{1}{2} k_{AB} \Delta_{AB}^2
$$

$$
U_{BC} = \frac{1}{2} k_{BC} \Delta_{BC}^2
$$

$$
\Delta = \Delta_{AB} + \Delta_{BC}
$$

$$
U_i = U_{AB} + U_{BC} = W (h + \Delta) = \frac{1}{2} k_{AB} \Delta_{AB}^2 + \frac{1}{2} k_{BC} \Delta_{BC}^2
$$

$$
2 W (h + \Delta) = k_{AB} \Delta_{AB}^2 + k_{BC} \Delta_{BC}^2
$$

$$
h + \Delta = \frac{k_{AB} \Delta_{AB}^2 + k_{BC} \Delta_{BC}^2}{2 W}
$$

$$
\Delta = \frac{k_{AB} \Delta_{AB}^2 + k_{BC} \Delta_{BC}^2}{2 W} - h
$$

So that's a system of equations:

$$
\Delta = \frac{k_{AB} \Delta_{AB}^2 + k_{BC} \Delta_{BC}^2}{2 W} - h
$$

$$
\Delta = \Delta_{AB} + \Delta_{BC}
$$

WolframAlpha, the tool of my choice does systems of equations and mixed units well, but not both. So I will be painstakingly converting everything to base units:

$$
k_{AB} = 3.666*10^8 N/m
$$

$$
k_{BC} = 7.333*10^8 N/m
$$

$$
W = 98.1N
$$

$$
h = 0.15m
$$

The system of equations (some substitutions for Greek letters because WolframAlpha doesn't like them):

$$
x = \frac{3.666*10^8 * y^2 + 7.333*10^8 N/m * z^2}{2 * 98.1} - 0.15
$$

$$
x = y + z
$$

$$
\Delta_{AB} = 0.2316mm
$$

$$
\Delta_{BC} = 0.1158mm
$$

$$
\Delta = 0.3474mm
$$

$$
\sigma_{AB} = \frac{F_{AB}}{A_{AB}} = \frac{k_{AB} \Delta_{AB}}{A_{AB}} = \frac{3.666*10^8 N/m * 0.2316mm}{314.2mm^2} = 270.2MPa
$$

$$
\sigma_{BC} = \frac{F_{BC}}{A_{BC}} = \frac{k_{BC} \Delta_{BC}}{A_{BC}} = \frac{7.333*10^8 N/m * 0.1158mm}{1257mm^2} = 67.55MPa
$$

$$
\sigma_{max} = \max(\sigma_{AB}, \sigma_{BC}) = \boxed{270.2MPa}
$$
