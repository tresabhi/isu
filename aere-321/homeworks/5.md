## 1.

Staring with the inertia:

$$
I = I_x = \int_A y^2 dA = 4 \int_0^a \left[ \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} (a - s) \right]^2 t ~ ds
$$

$$
I = 4 \frac{t}{2} \int_0^a (a^2 - 2as + s^2) ~ ds = 4 \frac{t}{2} \left[ a^2s - as^2 + \frac{1}{3} s^2 \right]_0^a
$$

$$
I = 4 \frac{t}{2} \left[ \frac{1}{3} a^3 \right] = 4 \frac{ta^3}{6} = \frac{2}{3} ta^3
$$

## 2.

Inertia:

$$
I = I_x = \frac{1}{12} t (2a)^3 + \cancel{\frac{1}{12} 2 t^3 a} + t a \left( \frac{a}{2} \right)^2
$$

$$
I = \frac{1}{12} t 8 a^3 + 2 t a \frac{a^2}{4} = \frac{2}{3} t a^3 + \frac{1}{2} t a ^3 = \frac{7}{6} t a^3
$$

The top web comes in from $y=a$ to $y=a/2$, so this is the shear flow:

$$
q_1 = \frac{V}{I} t \int_0^{s_1} y_1 ~ ds
$$

Translating $y_1$ to $s$ (doing top to down):

$$
y_1 = a - s
$$

Thus:

$$
q_1 = \frac{V}{I} t \int_0^{s_1} (a - s) ~ ds
$$

$$
q_1 = \frac{V}{I} t \left( a s_1 - \frac{1}{2} s_1^2 \right)
$$

$$
q_1 = \frac{6}{7} \frac{V}{ta^3} t \left( a s_1 - \frac{1}{2} s_1^2 \right)
$$

$$
\boxed{q_1 = \frac{6}{7} \frac{V}{a^3} \left( a s_1 - \frac{1}{2} s_1^2 \right)}
$$

The shear flow at $A$ due to the top web:

$$
q_{A1} = \frac{6}{7} \frac{V}{ta^3} t \left( a \frac{a}{2} - \frac{1}{2} \frac{a^2}{4} \right)
$$

$$
q_{A1} = \frac{6}{7} \frac{V}{a} \left( \frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{2} \frac{1}{4} \right)
$$

$$
q_{A1} = \frac{6}{7} \frac{V}{a} \frac{3}{8}
$$

$$
\boxed{q_{A1} = \frac{9 V}{28 a}}
$$

The top flange comes in from the right, horizontally:

$$
q_2 = \frac{V}{I} t \int_0^{s_2} y_2 ~ ds
$$

$y$ here is constant:

$$
y_2 = \frac{a}{2}
$$

$$
q_2 = \frac{V}{I} t \int_0^{s_2} \frac{a}{2} ~ ds
$$

$$
q_2 = \frac{1}{2} \frac{V}{I} t a s_2
$$

$$
q_2 = \frac{1}{2} \frac{6}{7} \frac{V}{t a^3} t a s_2
$$

$$
\boxed{q_2 = \frac{3}{7} \frac{V}{a^2} s_2}
$$

Shear flow at $A$ due to the top flange:

$$
q_2 = \frac{3}{7} \frac{V}{a^2} a
$$

$$
\boxed{q_2 = \frac{3 V}{7 a}}
$$
