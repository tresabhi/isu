Inertia about the $y$ axis:

$$
I_y = wl^3 + lw^3
$$

$$
I_y = 50mm * (200mm)^3 + 200mm * (50mm)^3 = 4.25*10^{-4}m^4
$$

I have placed the origin at the center of the lower block:

![](https://i.imgur.com/dEZUBshm.png)

This allows me to get the inertia about the $z$ axis:

$$
I_z = wl^3 + lw^3 + wl(l/2 + w/2)^2
$$

$$
I_z = 50mm * (200mm)^3 + 200mm * (50mm)^3 + 50mm * 200mm * (200mm / 2 + 50mm / 2)^2 = 5.813*10^8mm^4
$$

Decomposing the torque:

$$

M_z = M \cos \theta = 2kN m \cos 60\degree = 1 kN m
