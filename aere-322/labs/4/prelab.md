## 1.

$$
\sum F_y = F - w = 0 \implies F = w
$$

$$
\sum M_{x=0} = M - wL = 0 \implies M = wL
$$

---

$$
M(x) = -M \langle x - 0 \rangle^0 + F \langle x - 0 \rangle^1 - w \langle x - L \rangle^1
$$

$$
M(x) = F x - M = w x - w L
$$

$$
\theta(x) = \frac{1}{EI} \left[ \frac{1}{2} w x^2 - w L x \right] + C_1
$$

$$
\theta(x) = 0 = C_1
$$

$$
\theta(x) = \frac{1}{EI} \left[ \frac{1}{2} w x^2 - w L x \right]
$$

$$
y(x) = \frac{1}{EI} \left[ \frac{1}{6} w x^3 - \frac{1}{2} w L x^2 \right] + C_2
$$

$$
y(0) = 0 = C_2
$$

$$
y(x) = \frac{1}{EI} \left[ \frac{1}{6} w x^3 - \frac{1}{2} w L x^2 \right]
$$

---

$$
x = \frac{2}{3} L \implies y = \frac{1}{EI} \left[ \frac{1}{6} w \frac{8}{27} L^3 - \frac{1}{2} w L \frac{4}{9} L^2 \right]
$$

$$
y = - \frac{w}{EI} \frac{14}{81} L^3
$$
