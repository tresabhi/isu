# AERE 355 Cheat Sheet 1

## Trivial Preliminaries

Pure:

$$
\theta < 15\degree \implies \sin \theta \approx \theta
$$

$$
\delta(\theta) = \frac{\theta - \sin \theta}{\sin \theta}
$$

Bernoulli:

$$
P_1 + \frac{1}{2} \rho V_1^2 + \rho g z_1 = P_2 + \frac{1}{2} \rho V_2^2 + \rho g z_2
$$

Compressible fork:

$$
P = c \rho^\gamma
$$

$$
\gamma_{air} \approx 1.4
$$

$$
\frac{1}{2}V^2 + \frac{P \gamma}{\rho(\gamma - 1)} + \cancel{g z}^\text{often ignored} = C
$$

Atmosphere:

$$
dP = \rho g dh
$$

$$
g = g_0 \left( \frac{R_o}{R_o + h_G} \right)^2
$$

$$
h_G = \left( \frac{R_o}{R_o - h} \right) h
$$

$$
P = \rho R T
$$

$$
T = T_1 + \lambda (h - h_1)
$$

$$
\frac{P}{P_1}

= \left( \frac{T}{T_1} \right)^\frac{-g_o}{R \lambda}

= \frac{\rho T}{\rho_1 T_1}

= e^{\frac{-g_o}{R T_1} (h - h_1)}
$$

$$
\frac{\rho}{\rho_1}

= \left( \frac{T}{T_1} \right) ^ {-1 - \frac{g_o}{R \lambda}}

= \frac{\rho}{\rho_1}

= e^{\frac{-g_o}{R T_1} (h - h_1)}
$$

## Aerospace Preliminaries

$$
Q = \frac{1}{2} \rho V^2
$$

$$
C_L = C_{L_0} + C_{L_\alpha} \alpha
$$

$$
C_D = C_{D_{min}} + K C_L^2
$$

$$
\begin{align*}
  C_{L_\alpha} &= \frac{C_{l_\alpha}}{1 + \frac{C_{l_\alpha}}{\pi AR}} \quad \text{(for radians)} \\
  &= \frac{C_{l_\alpha}}{1 + 57.3 \frac{C_{l_\alpha}}{\pi AR}} \quad \text{(for degrees)}
\end{align*}
$$

$$
AR = \frac{b^2}{S}
$$

## Aircraft Dynamics

![](https://i.imgur.com/QSMEKkrm.png) ![](https://i.imgur.com/jS9qgnMm.png) ![](https://i.imgur.com/ReeqIrQm.png)

Lengths:

$$
h = \frac{x_cg}{\bar{c}}
$$

$$
h_{ac} = \frac{x_{ac}}{\bar{c}}
$$

$$
h_{ac_w} = h_{ac}
$$

Wing moments:

$$
\begin{align*}
  C_m &= C_{m_{ac}} + C_L (h - h_{ac}) \\
  &= C_{m_{ac}} + (C_{L_0} + C_{L_\alpha} \alpha) (h - h_{ac}) \\
  &= [C_{m_{ac}} + C_{L_0} (h - h_{ac})] + [C_{L_\alpha} (h - h_{ac})] \alpha \\
  &= C_{m_0} + C_{m_\alpha} \alpha
\end{align*}
$$

$$
C_{m_0} = C_{m_{ac}} + C_{L_0} (h - h_{ac})
$$

$$
C_{m_\alpha} = \frac{d C_m}{d \alpha} = C_{L_\alpha} (h - h_{ac})
$$

$$
C_{m_w} = C_{m_{0_w}} + C_{m_{\alpha_w}} \alpha
$$

$$
C_{m_{0_w}} = C_{m_{ac_w}} + C_{L_{0_w}} (h - h_{ac})
$$

$$
C_{m_{\alpha_w}} = C_{L_{\alpha_w}} (h - h_{ac})
$$

$$
C_{L_w} = C_{L_{0_w}} + C_{L_{\alpha_w}} \alpha
$$

Tail moments:

$$
M_t = -L_t l_t = -C_{L_t} Q_t S_t l_t
$$

$$
C_{m_t} = C_{m_{0_t}} + C_{m_{\alpha_t}} \alpha
$$

$$
C_{m_{0_t}} = \eta V_H C_{L_{\alpha_t}} (\epsilon_0 + i_w - i_t)
$$

$$
C_{m_{\alpha_t}} = -\eta V_H C_{L_{\alpha_t}} (1 - \frac{d\epsilon}{d\alpha})
$$

$$
\eta = \frac{V_t^2 / 2}{V_w^2 / 2} = \frac{Q_t}{Q_w} \approx 1 \quad \text{approximation for most cases}
$$

$$
V_H = \frac{l_t S_t}{\bar{c} S}
$$

$$
\begin{align*}
  \epsilon_0 &= 57.3 \frac{2 C_{L_{0_w}}}{\pi AR_w} \quad \text{for degrees} \\
  &= \frac{2 C_{L_{0_w}}}{\pi AR_w} \quad \text{for radians}
\end{align*}
$$

$$
\begin{align*}
  \frac{d\epsilon}{d\alpha} &= 57.3 \frac{2 C_{L_{\alpha_w}}}{\pi AR_w} \quad \text{for degrees} \\
  &= \frac{2 C_{L_{\alpha_w}}}{\pi AR_w} \quad \text{for radians}
\end{align*}
$$

$$
C_{L_t} = C_{L_{\alpha_t}} \alpha_t = C_{L_{\alpha_t}} (\alpha_w - (i_w - i_t) - \epsilon)
$$

Airplane moments:

Passive stability:

$$
C_{m_\alpha} < 0 \cup C_{m_0} > 0
$$

Lifts:

$$
\begin{align*}
  \epsilon &= 57.3 \frac{2 C_{L_w}}{\pi AR_w} \quad \text{for degrees} \\
  &= \epsilon_0 + \frac{d\epsilon}{d\alpha_w} \alpha_w = \frac{2 C_{L_w}}{\pi AR_w} \quad \text{for radians}
\end{align*}
$$

$$
\alpha_w = \alpha
$$
