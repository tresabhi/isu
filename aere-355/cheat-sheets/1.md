# AERE 355 Cheat Sheet 1

## Trivial Preliminaries

Pure:

$$
\theta < 15\degree \implies \sin \theta \approx \theta
$$

$$
\delta(\theta) = \frac{\theta - \sin \theta}{\sin \theta}
$$

Bernoulli:

$$
P_1 + \frac{1}{2} \rho V_1^2 + \rho g z_1 = P_2 + \frac{1}{2} \rho V_2^2 + \rho g z_2
$$

Compressible fork:

$$
P = c \rho^\gamma
$$

$$
\gamma_{air} \approx 1.4
$$

$$
\frac{1}{2}V^2 + \frac{P \gamma}{\rho(\gamma - 1)} + \cancel{g z}^\text{often ignored} = C
$$

Atmosphere:

$$
dP = \rho g dh
$$

$$
g = g_0 \left( \frac{R_o}{R_o + h_G} \right)^2
$$

$$
h_G = \left( \frac{R_o}{R_o - h} \right) h
$$

$$
P = \rho R T
$$

$$
T = T_1 + \lambda (h - h_1)
$$

$$
\frac{P}{P_1}

= \left( \frac{T}{T_1} \right)^\frac{-g_o}{R \lambda}

= \frac{\rho T}{\rho_1 T_1}

= e^{\frac{-g_o}{R T_1} (h - h_1)}
$$

$$
\frac{\rho}{\rho_1}

= \left( \frac{T}{T_1} \right) ^ {-1 - \frac{g_o}{R \lambda}}

= \frac{\rho}{\rho_1}

= e^{\frac{-g_o}{R T_1} (h - h_1)}
$$

## Aircraft Dynamics

![](https://i.imgur.com/QSMEKkrm.png) ![](https://i.imgur.com/jS9qgnMm.png) ![](https://i.imgur.com/ReeqIrQm.png) ![](https://i.imgur.com/r8LDX3Nl.png)

Lengths:

$$
h = \frac{x_cg}{\bar{c}}
$$

$$
h_{ac} = \frac{x_{ac}}{\bar{c}}
$$

$$
h_{ac_w} = h_{ac}
$$

Wing moments:

$$
\begin{align*}
  C_m
  &= C_{m_{ac}} + C_L (h - h_{ac}) \\
  &= C_{m_{ac}} + (C_{L_0} + C_{L_\alpha} \alpha) (h - h_{ac}) \\
  &= [C_{m_{ac}} + C_{L_0} (h - h_{ac})] + [C_{L_\alpha} (h - h_{ac})] \alpha \\
  &= C_{m_0} + C_{m_\alpha} \alpha
\end{align*}
$$

$$
C_{m_0} = C_{m_{ac}} + C_{L_0} (h - h_{ac})
$$

$$
C_{m_\alpha} = \frac{d C_m}{d \alpha} = C_{L_\alpha} (h - h_{ac})
$$

$$
C_{m_w} = C_{m_{0_w}} + C_{m_{\alpha_w}} \alpha
$$

$$
C_{m_{0_w}} = C_{m_{ac_w}} + C_{L_{0_w}} (h - h_{ac})
$$

$$
C_{m_{\alpha_w}} = C_{L_{\alpha_w}} (h - h_{ac})
$$

$$
C_{L_w} = C_{L_{0_w}} + C_{L_{\alpha_w}} \alpha
$$

Tail moments:

$$
M_t = -L_t l_t = -C_{L_t} Q_t S_t l_t
$$

$$
\begin{align*}
  C_{m_t}
  &= C_{m_{0_t}} + C_{m_{\alpha_t}} \alpha \\
  &= \frac{M_t}{Q S \bar{c}} \\
  &= -\eta V_H C_{L_t} \\
  &= -\eta \left[ \bar{V}_H - \frac{S_t}{S} (h - h_{ac}) \right] C_{L_t}
\end{align*}
$$

$$
C_{m_{0_t}} = \eta V_H C_{L_{\alpha_t}} (\epsilon_0 + i_w - i_t)
$$

$$
C_{m_{\alpha_t}} = -\eta V_H C_{L_{\alpha_t}} (1 - \frac{d\epsilon}{d\alpha})
$$

$$
\eta = \frac{V_t^2 / 2}{V_w^2 / 2} = \frac{Q_t}{Q_w} \approx 1 \quad \text{approximation for most cases}
$$

$$
\begin{align*}
  V_H
  &= \frac{l_t S_t}{\bar{c} S} \\
  &= \left[ \frac{\bar{l}_t}{\bar{c}} - (h - h_{ac}) \right] \frac{S_t}{S} \\
  &= \frac{\bar{l}_t S_t}{\bar{c} S} - \frac{S_t}{S} (h - h_{ac}) \\
  &= \bar{V}_H - \frac{S_t}{S} (h - h_{ac})
\end{align*}
$$

$$
\frac{l_t}{\bar{c}} = h_{ac_t} - h = \frac{\bar{l}_t}{\bar{c}} - (h - h_{ac})
$$

$$
\begin{align*}
  \epsilon_0
  &= 57.3 \frac{2 C_{L_{0_w}}}{\pi AR_w} &\text{for degrees} \\
  &= \frac{2 C_{L_{0_w}}}{\pi AR_w} &\text{for radians}
\end{align*}
$$

$$
\begin{align*}
  \frac{d\epsilon}{d\alpha}
  &= 57.3 \frac{2 C_{L_{\alpha_w}}}{\pi AR_w} &\text{for degrees} \\
  &= \frac{2 C_{L_{\alpha_w}}}{\pi AR_w} &\text{for radians}
\end{align*}
$$

$$
C_{L_t} = C_{L_{\alpha_t}} \alpha_t = C_{L_{\alpha_t}} (\alpha_w - (i_w - i_t) - \epsilon)
$$

Airplane moments:

$$
\begin{align*}
  C_m
  &= C_{m_0} + C_{m_\alpha} \alpha \\
  &= (C_{m_{0_w}} + C_{m_{0_t}}) + (C_{m_{\alpha_w}} + C_{m_{\alpha_t}}) \alpha \\
  &= C_{m_w} + C_{m_t} \\
  &= C_{m_{ac_w}} + \left[ C_{L_w} + \eta \frac{S_t}{S} C_{L_t} \right] (h - h_{ac}) - \eta \bar{V}_H C_{L_t} \\
  &= C_{m_{ac_w}} + C_L (h - h_{ac}) - \bar{V}_H C_{L_t} \impliedby \eta \approx 1 \\\\

  C_{m_0} &= C_{m_{0_w}} + C_{m_{0_t}} \\
  &= \left[ C_{m_{ac_w}} + C_{L_{0_w}} (h - h_{ac}) \right] + \left[ \eta V_H C_{L_{\alpha_t}} (\epsilon_0 + i_w - i_t) \right] \\\\

  C_{m_\alpha} &= C_{m_{\alpha_w}} + C_{m_{\alpha_t}} \\
  &= \left[ C_{L_{\alpha_w}} (h - h_{ac}) \right] + \left[ -\eta V_H C_{L_{\alpha_t}} \left( 1 - \frac{d\epsilon}{d\alpha} \right) \right] \\
  &= C_{L_\alpha} (h - h_{ac}) - \bar{V}_H \frac{d C_{L_t}}{d \alpha} \\
  &= C_{L_\alpha} (h - h_n)
\end{align*}
$$

$$
\begin{align*}
  \frac{d C_m(\alpha)}{d\alpha} &= C_{m_\alpha} = 0 \impliedby \text{Stick fixed neutrality}
\end{align*}
$$

$$
\begin{align*}
  h_n
  &= h - C_{m_\alpha} / C_{L_\alpha} \\
  &= h - dC_m / dC_L \\
  &= h_{ac} + \frac{\eta V_H C_{L_{\alpha_t}}}{C_{L_{\alpha_w}}} \left( 1 - \frac{d\epsilon}{a\alpha} \right) \impliedby \text{Stick fixed neutrality}
\end{align*}
$$

Passive stability:

$$
K_n = h_n - h > 0

  &= \left[ C_{L_{0_w}} - \eta \frac{S_t}{S} C_{L_{\alpha_t}} (\epsilon_0 + i_w - i_t) \right] + \left[ C_{L_{\alpha_w}} + C_{L_{\alpha_t}} \eta \frac{S_t}{S} \left( 1 - \frac{d\epsilon}{d\alpha} \right) \right] \alpha

Rudder:

![](https://i.imgur.com/ugzKenK.png) ![](https://i.imgur.com/GngtBQYm.png)

$$
C_{n_\beta} > 0 \implies \text{Weathercock stability}
$$

$$
C_n = C_{n_\beta} \beta + C_{n_{\delta_r}} \delta_r + C_{n_{\delta_a}} \delta_a
$$

$$
C_{n_\beta} = C_{n_{\beta_{wf}}} + C_{n_{\beta_v}}
$$

$$
C_{n_{\beta_{wf}}} = -k_n k_{Rl} \frac{S_{fs} l_f}{S b} \quad \text{In /deg}
$$

$$
\begin{align*}
  S_{fs} &= \text{Projected side area of the fuselage} \\
  l_f &= \text{Total length of the fuselage (from nose tip to back of tail)} \\
  R_{l_f} &= \text{Body Reynold's number} \\
  V &= \text{Free stream velocity} \\
  v &= \text{Kinematic viscosity} \\
  x_m &= \text{Distance from the tip of the nose to the plane cg}
\end{align*}
$$

1. One begins with knowledge of $x_m / l_f$, where $x_m$ is the distance from the tip of the nose to the plane cg.
2. Knowledge of $l_f^2 / S_{fs}$ gives the first turn of the path.
3. Knowledge of the shown fuselage height ratio $\sqrt{h_1 / h_2}$ gives the second turn.
4. Knowledge of the ratio $h / w_f$ completes the third turn, allowing one to then estimate $k_n$ in the fourth and final leg.

$$
R_{l_f} = V \frac{l_f}{v}
$$

Vertical stabilizer:

$$
Y_v = - C_{L_{\alpha_v}} \alpha_v Q_v S_v \quad \text{Side force on vertical stabilizer}
$$

![](https://i.imgur.com/Rw00nfEm.png)

$$
\alpha_v = \beta + \sigma \quad \text{Sidewash angle is} ~ \sigma
$$

$$
C_{L_v} = C_{L_{\alpha_v}} \alpha_v = C_{L_{\alpha_v}} (\beta + \sigma)
$$

$$
C_{L_\beta} = C_{L_{\alpha_v}} \left( 1 + \frac{\partial \sigma}{\partial \beta} \right)
$$

$$
C_{L_{\alpha_v}} > 0 \implies \text{Passive stability}
$$

$$
N_v = -l_v Y_v = l_v C_{L_{\alpha_v}} (\beta + \sigma) Q_v S_v
$$

$$
C_{n_v} = \frac{N_v}{Q_w S b} = V_v \eta_v C_{L_{\alpha_v}} (\beta + \sigma)
$$

$$
V_v = \frac{l_v S_v}{S b}
$$

$$
\eta_n = \frac{Q_v}{Q_w}
$$

$$
\begin{align*}
  C_{n_{\beta_v}}
  &= V_v \eta_v C_{L_\beta} \\
  &= V_v \eta_v C_{L_{\alpha_v}} \left( 1 + \frac{\partial \sigma}{\partial \beta} \right)
\end{align*}
$$

$$
C_{n_{\beta_v}} > 0 \implies \text{Passive stability}
$$

$$
\eta_v \left( 1 + \frac{\partial \sigma}{\partial \beta} \right) = 0.724 + 3.06 \frac{S_v / S}{1 + \cos \Lambda_{c/4w}} + 0.4 \frac{z_w}{d} + 0.009 AR_w
$$

$$
C_{L_r} = C_{L_{\delta_r}} \delta_r
$$

$$
C_{n_r} = - V_v \eta_v C_{L_r} = -V_v \eta_v C_{L_{\delta_r}} \delta_r = C_{n_{\delta_r}} \delta_r
$$

$$
C_{L_{\delta_r}} = C_{L_{\alpha_v}} \tau = \frac{C_{L_\beta} \tau}{1 + \frac{\partial \sigma}{\partial \beta}}
$$

$$
C_{L_\beta} = C_{L_{\alpha_v}} \left( 1 + \frac{\partial \sigma}{\partial \beta} \right)
$$

$$
\tau = -\frac{C_{n_{\delta_r}}}{C_{n_{\beta_v}}} \left( 1 + \frac{\partial \sigma}{\partial \beta} \right)
$$

$$
\frac{\partial \sigma}{\partial \beta} = -1 - \frac{\tau C_{n_{\beta_v}}}{C_{n_{\delta_r}}}
$$
