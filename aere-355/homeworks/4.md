# AERE 355 Homework 4

## 1.

I will be using the equations provided in ME 345 to solve the spring system which are pretty much the same as the ones from this class.

Given:

$$
m = 5 lbm = 0.1554 slug
$$

$$
c_a = 0
$$

$$
c_b = 0.6 \frac{lb*s}{ft}
$$

Supplementary:

$$
k = 60 lb/ft
$$

$$
y(0) = 0
$$

$$
\dot{y}(0) = 10 ft/s
$$

Standard form:

$$
\ddot{y} + 2 \zeta \omega_n \dot{y} + \omega_n^2 y = 0
$$

Solved for $y$:

$$
\omega_n^2 y = -\ddot{y} - 2 \zeta \omega_n \dot{y}
$$

$$
y = - \frac{1}{\omega_n^2} \ddot{y} - \frac{2 \zeta}{\omega_n} \dot{y}
$$

The coefficients:

$$
\omega_n = \sqrt{\frac{k}{m}} = \sqrt{\frac{60lb/ft}{0.1554slug}} = 19.65 rad/s
$$

$$
\zeta_a = 0 \impliedby c_a = 0
$$

$$
\zeta_b = \frac{c_b}{2 m \omega_n} = \frac{0.6 \frac{lb*s}{ft}}{2 * 0.1554slug * 19.65 rad/s} = 0.09824
$$

$$
\omega_{da} = \omega_n = 19.65 rad/s \impliedby \zeta_a = 0
$$

$$
\omega_{db} = \omega_n \sqrt{1 - \zeta^2} = 19.65 rad/s \sqrt{1 - 0.09824^2} = 19.56 rad/s
$$

$y$ as a function of time:

$$
y(t) = C \exp(-\zeta \omega_n t) \sin(\omega_d t + \psi)
$$

$$
\dot{y}(t) = -\zeta \omega_n C \exp(-\zeta \omega_n t) \sin(\omega_d t + \psi) + \omega_d C \exp(-\zeta \omega_n t) \cos(\omega_d t + \psi)
$$

Initial conditions:

$$
y(0) = C \sin \psi
$$

$$
\dot{y}(0) = -\zeta \omega_n C \sin \psi + \omega_d C \cos \psi
$$

System of equations for no damping:

$$
C_a \sin \psi_a = 0
$$

$$
19.65 C_a \cos \psi_a = 10
$$

$$
C_a = 0.5089 ft, \psi_a = 0
$$

Final equation for no damping:

$$
\boxed{y_a(t) = 0.5089 ft * \sin(19.65 s^{-1} * t)}
$$

As for the damped system:

$$
C_b \sin \psi_b = 0
$$

$$
-0.09824 * 19.65 * C_b \sin \psi_b + 19.56 * C_b \cos \psi_b = 10
$$

$$
C_b = 0.5112 ft, \psi_b = 0
$$

$$
y_b(t) = 0.5112 ft * \exp(-0.09824 * 19.65 s^{-1} * t) \sin(19.56 s^{-1} * t)
$$

$$
\boxed{y_b(t) = 0.5112 ft * \exp(-1.930 s^{-1} * t) \sin(19.56 s^{-1} * t)}
$$
