# AERE 355 Homework 5

## 1.

Given:

$$
\dot{\alpha} + 2 \alpha - q = 0
$$

$$
\ddot{\theta} + 10 \alpha + 150 = -5 \delta
$$

Substitutions:

$$
x_1 = \alpha
$$

$$
x_2 = \dot{\alpha}
$$

$$
x_3 = \theta
$$

$$
x_4 = \dot{\theta}
$$

Rewriting:

$$
x_2 + 2 x_1 - q = 0
$$

$$
\dot{x}_1 = -2 x_1 + q
$$

$$
\dot{x}_2 = -2 x_2
$$

$$
\dot{x}_3 = x_4
$$

$$
\dot{x}_4 + 10 x_1 + 150 = -5 \delta
$$

$$
\dot{x}_4 = -10 x_1 - 150 - 5 \delta
$$

Vector forms:

$$
X = \begin{bmatrix}
  x_1 \\
  x_2 \\
  x_3 \\
  x_4
\end{bmatrix}
$$

$$
\dot{X} = \begin{bmatrix}
  \dot{x}_1 \\
  \dot{x}_2 \\
  \dot{x}_3 \\
  \dot{x}_4
\end{bmatrix}
$$

$$
U = \begin{bmatrix}
  q \\
  \delta \\
  1
\end{bmatrix}
$$

Final form:

$$
\dot{X} = \begin{bmatrix}
  -2 x_1 + q \\
  -2 x_2 \\
  x_4 \\
  -10 x_1 - 150 - 5 \delta
\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}
  -2 & 0 & 0 & 0 \\
  0 & -2 & 0 & 0 \\
  0 & 0 & 0 & 1 \\
  -10 & 0 & 0 & 0
\end{bmatrix} X + \begin{bmatrix}
  1 & 0 \\
  0 & 0 \\
  0 & 0 \\
  0 & -5
\end{bmatrix} U + \begin{bmatrix}
  0 \\
  0 \\
  0 \\
  -150
\end{bmatrix}
$$
