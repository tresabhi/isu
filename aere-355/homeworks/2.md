## 2.

Given:

$$
x_{cg} / \bar{c} = h = 0.3
$$

$$
C_{m_{cg}} = C_{m_0} + \frac{d C_{m_{cg}}}{d C_L} C_L + C_{m_{\delta_e}} \delta_e
$$

$$
C_{m_0} = 0.05
$$

$$
\frac{d C_{m_{cg}}}{d C_L} = -0.1
$$

$$
C_{m_{\delta_e}} = -0.01/\deg
$$

$$
\frac{d C_{m_{cg}}}{d C_L} = \frac{x_{cg}}{\bar{c}} - \frac{x_{np}}{\bar{c}} = h - h_n
$$

$$
\delta_{e_{max}} = \pm 20 \degree
$$

Landing condition:

$$
x_{cg}^{new} / \bar{c} = h^{new} = 0.10
$$

$$
C_L = 1.0
$$

The questions is regarding wether the airplane can be trimmed or not and I have a feeling it can. I will be using the following equation which of course will be $0$:

$$
C_{m_{cg}} = C_{m_0} + \frac{d C_{m_{cg}}}{d C_L} C_L + C_{m_{\delta_e}} \delta_e
$$

$$
0 = C_{m_0} + \frac{d C_{m_{cg}}}{d C_L} C_L + C_{m_{\delta_e}} \delta_e
$$

$$
-C_{m_{\delta_e}} \delta_e = C_{m_0} + \frac{d C_{m_{cg}}}{d C_L} C_L
$$

$$
\delta_e = \frac{1}{C_{m_{\delta_e}}} \left[ -C_{m_0} - \frac{d C_{m_{cg}}}{d C_L} C_L \right]
$$

Everything is known here or given, except the derivative fraction, which is:

$$
\frac{d C_{m_{cg}}}{d C_L} = h - h_n
$$

$$
\delta_e = \frac{1}{C_{m_{\delta_e}}} \left[ -C_{m_0} - (h - h_n) C_L \right]
$$

$$
\delta_e = \frac{(h_n - h) C_L - C_{m_0}}{C_{m_{\delta_e}}}
$$

All I must find is $h_n$ here. This can be found from the initial condition:

$$
\frac{d C_{m_{cg}}}{d C_L} = h - h_n
$$

$$
h_n = h - \frac{d C_{m_{cg}}}{d C_L}
$$

$$
h_n = 0.3 - (-0.1) = 0.4
$$

Back to the original equation:

$$
\delta_e = \frac{(h_n - h^{new}) C_L - C_{m_0}}{C_{m_{\delta_e}}}
$$

$$
\delta_e = \frac{(0.4 - 0.10) * 1.0 - 0.05}{-0.01/\deg} = \boxed{-25\degree}
$$

Well, my instincts were wrong. The trim of $\delta_e = -25\degree < 20\degree$ exceeds the airplane capabilities. I advice the pilot to find the cobra button.
