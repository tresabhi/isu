# AERE 490 Report 8

In project 4, I attempted to replicate what I think Psyche, the asteroid, looks like today, and this is what I came up with:

![](https://i.imgur.com/CjAjRJp.jpeg)

There's a corresponding GIF that goes with the model. PDFs don't supported embedding GIFs, so here's the link: https://i.imgur.com/d4viVGj.gif

The shape of my model isn't arbitrary. The overall shape is based on the paper/letter _(16) Psyche: A mesosiderite-like asteroid?_ by M. Viikinkoski et. al. In the paper, several images from VLT, SPHERE, and ZIMPOL are presented, with deconvolved models generate by Mistral, as seen below.

![](https://www.aanda.org/articles/aa/full_html/2018/11/aa34091-18/aa34091-18-fig1.jpg)

Based on this model and my best guess of what the surface should look like (fairly exposed metal at rims of craters and a lot of regolith-like coverage everywhere else), I came up with the model that you see above, made in Blender using Geometry Nodes and Shader Nodes.

I also decided to turn this into a 3D-printable .STL file... which I may or may not have left as a 1:1 scale. That's 140 miles in diameter! PrusaSlicer seems to scale it down to fit within the selected printer, which is nice I suppose. However, I can't get the idea that this looks like broccoli out of my head.
