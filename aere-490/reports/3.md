In week 6, we were introduced to Haley Bates-Tarasewicz, the Psyche Payload Systems Engineer at JPL. Our meeting was held on Friday, October 31st, which was both Halloween and Ms. Tarasewicz’s birthday, who is a big fan of costumes, making it the perfect combination for her.

I quizzed Ms. Tarasewicz on the dilemma of picking instrumentation aboard missions, to which I was provided two different approaches.

Every decade, scientists across the country complete the Decadal Survey, which documents the state of science and highlights the burning questions that NASA can address using its capabilities. These survey results will inform the NASA board on directing new missions with specific instrumentation.

The other kind of mission is more anarchist to my liking, where a primary mission offers scientists the ability to nominate instrumentation that will be taken along the primary mission. This can happen aboard the Decadal missions or missions powered by other entities, guided by NASA. There, it’s tough to sus out what makes it and what doesn’t as it’s up to the primary investigators.
