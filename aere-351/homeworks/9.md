# AERE 351 Homework 9

## 1.

From the Internet:

$$
R = 6378km
$$

$$
\mu = 3.98600 * 10^5 km^3/s^2
$$

Given:

$$
h_p = 250km
$$

$$
v_\infty = 3.5km/s
$$

$$
i = 15\deg
$$

$$
\Omega = 178\deg
$$

$$
\omega = 25\deg
$$

Actual radius:

$$
r_p = R + h_p = 6378km + 250km = 6628 km
$$

Semi major axis:

$$
v_\infty = \sqrt{\frac{\mu}{-a}}
$$

$$
a = -\frac{\mu}{v_\infty^2} = - \frac{3.98600 * 10^5 km^3/s^2}{(3.5km/s)^2} = \boxed{-32500 km}
$$

Eccentricity:

$$
r_p = a (e - 1) = a e - a
$$

$$
r_p + a = a e
$$

$$
e = \frac{r_p}{a} + 1 = \frac{6628km}{-32500km} + 1 = \boxed{0.7961}
$$
