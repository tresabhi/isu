## 1.

![](https://i.imgur.com/reszTkC.png)

From the Internet:

$$
\mu = 3.98600 * 10^5 km^3/s^2
$$

$$
R = 6378km
$$

Given:

$$
h_1 = 300km
$$

$$
r_1 = h_1 + R = 6678km
$$

$$
e_1 = 0
$$

$$
r_{p2} = r_1 = 6678km
$$

$$
e_2 = 0.3
$$

$$
r_{a2} = r_{a3}
$$

$$
h_4 = 3000km
$$

$$
r_{p3} = r_4 = h_4 + R = 9378km
$$

Preliminaries:

$$
a_1 = \frac{r_{a1} + r_{p1}}{2} = r_1 = 6678km
$$

$$
p_1 = a_1 (1 - e_1^2) = a_1 = 6678km
$$

Velocities:

$$
v_1 = v_{\theta 1} = \sqrt{\frac{\mu}{p_1}} \cancel{(1 + e_1 \cos \theta)} = \sqrt{\frac{3.98600 * 10^5 km^3/s^2}{6678km}} = 7.726 km/s
$$

$$
e_2 = \frac{r_{a2} - r_{p2}}{r_{a2} + r_{p2}} = 0.3 = \frac{r_{a2} - 6678km}{r_{a2} + 6678km} \implies r_{a2} = 12402km
$$

$$
a_2 = \frac{r_{a2} + r_{p2}}{2} = \frac{12402km + 6678km}{2} = 9540km
$$

$$
p_2 = a_2 (1 - e_2^2) = 9540km * (1 - 0.3^2) = 8681.4km
$$

$$
v_{p2} = \sqrt{\frac{\mu}{p_2}} (1 + e_2) = \sqrt{\frac{3.98600 * 10^5 km^3/s^2}{8681.4km}} (1 + 0.3) = 8.809 km/s
$$

$$
v_{a2} = \sqrt{\frac{\mu}{p_2}} (1 - e_2) = \sqrt{\frac{3.98600 * 10^5 km^3/s^2}{8681.4km}} (1 - 0.3) = 4.743 km/s
$$

$$
r_{a3} = r_{a2} = 12402km
$$

$$
e_3 = \frac{r_{a3} - r_{p3}}{r_{a3} + r_{p3}} = \frac{12402km - 9378km}{12402km + 9378km} = 0.1388
$$

$$
a_3 = \frac{r_{a3} + r_{p3}}{2} = \frac{12402km + 9378km}{2} = 10890km
$$

$$
p_3 = a_3 (1 - e_3^2) = 10890km * (1 - 0.1388^2) = 10680km
$$

$$
v_{a3} = \sqrt{\frac{\mu}{p_3}} (1 - e_3) = \sqrt{\frac{3.98600 * 10^5 km^3/s^2}{10680km}} (1 - 0.1388) = 5.26123 km/s
$$

$$
v_{p3} = \sqrt{\frac{\mu}{p_3}} (1 + e_3) = \sqrt{\frac{3.98600 * 10^5 km^3/s^2}{10680km}} (1 + 0.1388) = 6.957 km/s
$$

$$
p_4 = a_4 = r_4 = 9378km
$$

$$
v_4 = \sqrt{\frac{\mu}{p_4}} = \sqrt{\frac{3.98600 * 10^5 km^3/s^2}{9378km}} = 6.520 km/s
$$

$$
\Delta v_A = v_{p2} - v_1 = 8.809 km/s - 7.726 km/s = 1.083 km/s
$$

$$
\Delta v_B = v_{a3} - v_{a2} = 5.26123 km/s - 4.743 km/s = 0.5182 km/s
$$

$$
\Delta v_C = v_{p3} - v_4 = 6.957 km/s - 6.520 km/s = 0.437 km/s
$$

$$
\Delta v = \Delta v_A + \Delta v_B + \Delta v_C = 1.083 km/s + 0.5182 km/s + 0.437 km/s = \boxed{2.038 km/s}
$$

$$
T = 2 \pi \sqrt{\frac{a^3}{\mu}}
$$

$$
\Delta T = \frac{1}{2} T = \pi \sqrt{\frac{a^3}{\mu}}
$$

$$
\Delta T_2 = \pi \sqrt{\frac{a_2^3}{\mu}} = \pi \sqrt{\frac{(9540km)^3}{3.98600 * 10^5 km^3/s^2}} = 4637s
$$

$$
\Delta T_3 = \pi \sqrt{\frac{a_3^3}{\mu}} = \pi \sqrt{\frac{(10890km)^3}{3.98600 * 10^5 km^3/s^2}} = 5655 seconds
$$

$$
\Delta T = \Delta T_2 + \Delta T_3 = 4637s + 5655s = \boxed{10292s}
$$

## 2.

![](https://i.imgur.com/PAe9f5x.png)

Given:

$$
h = 350km
$$

$$
N = 1
$$

From the Internet:

$$
R = 6378km
$$

$$
\mu = 3.98600 * 10^5 km^3/s^2
$$

Finding the true radius:

$$
r = h + R = 350km + 6378km = 6728km
$$

Some geometry:

$$
\Delta c = 600km
$$

$$
c = 2 \pi r \implies \Delta c = r \Delta \theta \implies \Delta \theta = \frac{\Delta c}{r} = \frac{600km}{6728km} = 0.08918 rad
$$

This change in $\theta$ should propagate all the way to $t$:

$$
\tan \frac{\Delta E}{2} = \sqrt{\frac{1 - e}{1 + e}} \tan \frac{\Delta \theta}{2}
$$

$$
e = 0 \implies a = p = r = 6728km
$$

$$
\tan \frac{\Delta E}{2} = \cancel{\sqrt{\frac{1 - e}{1 + e}}} \tan \frac{\Delta \theta}{2}
$$

$$
\tan \frac{\Delta E}{2} = \tan \frac{\Delta \theta}{2}
$$

$$
\Delta E = \Delta \theta = 0.08918 rad
$$

$$
M = E - \cancel{e \sin E} = E
$$

$$
\Delta M = \Delta E = 0.08918 rad
$$

$$
\Delta t = \sqrt{\frac{a^3}{\mu}} \Delta M = \sqrt{\frac{(6728km)^3}{3.98600 * 10^5 km^3/s^2}} * 0.08918 = 77.95s
$$

Time of original orbit:

$$
T = 2 \pi \sqrt{\frac{a^3}{\mu}} = 2 \pi \sqrt{\frac{(6728km)^3}{3.98600 * 10^5 km^3/s^2}} = 5492s
$$

Slow down to speed up and vice versa:

$$
T_A = T - \Delta t = 5492s - 77.95s = \boxed{5414s}
$$

$$
T_B = T + \Delta t = 5492s + 77.95s = \boxed{5570s}
$$

Time to figure out $r_a$ and $r_p$ for $A$ and $B$:

$$
T = 2 \pi \sqrt{\frac{a^3}{\mu}}
$$

$$
\frac{T}{2 \pi} = \sqrt{\frac{a^3}{\mu}}
$$

$$
\frac{T^2}{4 \pi^2} = \frac{a^3}{\mu}
$$

$$
a = \left( \frac{\mu T^2}{4 \pi^2} \right)^{1/3}
$$

$$
a_A = \left( \frac{\mu T_A^2}{4 \pi^2} \right)^{1/3} = \left( \frac{3.98600 * 10^5 km^3/s^2 * (5414s)^2}{4 \pi^2} \right)^{1/3} = 6664km
$$

$$
a_B = \left( \frac{\mu T_B^2}{4 \pi^2} \right)^{1/3} = \left( \frac{3.98600 * 10^5 km^3/s^2 * (5570s)^2}{4 \pi^2} \right)^{1/3} = 6791km
$$

$$
a = \frac{r_a + r_p}{2}
$$

$$
r_a = 2a - r_p
$$

$$
r_p = 2a - r_a
$$

$$
r_{aA} = r_{pB} = r = 6728km
$$

$$
r_{pA} = 2a_A - r_{aA} = 2 * 6664km - 6728km = 6600 km
$$

$$
r_{aB} = 2a_B - r_{pB} = 2 * 6791km - 6728km = 6854 km
$$

$$
e_A = \frac{r_{aA} - r_{pA}}{r_{aA} + r_{pA}} = \frac{6728km - 6600km}{6728km + 6600km} = \boxed{0.009604}
$$

$$
e_B = \frac{r_{aB} - r_{pB}}{r_{aB} + r_{pB}} = \frac{6854km - 6728km}{6854km + 6728km} = \boxed{0.009277}
$$

$$
p_A = a_A (1 - e_A^2) = 6664km * (1 - 0.009604^2) = 6663 km
$$

$$
p_B = a_B (1 - e_B^2) = 6791km * (1 - 0.009277^2) = 6790 km
$$

$$
p = r = 6728km
$$

$$
v = \sqrt{\frac{\mu}{p}} = \sqrt{\frac{3.98600 * 10^5 km^3/s^2}{6728km}} = 7.697 km/s
$$

$$
v_A = \sqrt{\frac{\mu}{p_A}} (1 - e_A) = \sqrt{\frac{3.98600 * 10^5 km^3/s^2}{6663km}} (1 - 0.009604) = 7.660 km/s
$$

$$
v_B = \sqrt{\frac{\mu}{p_B}} (1 + e_A) = \sqrt{\frac{3.98600 * 10^5 km^3/s^2}{6790km}} (1 + 0.009277) = 7.733 km/s
$$

$$
\Delta v_A = v - v_A = 7.697 km/s - 7.660 km/s = \boxed{0.037 km/s}
$$

$$
\Delta v_B = v_B - v = 7.733 km/s - 7.697 km/s = \boxed{0.036 km/s}
$$

Oddly similar? But I suppose that makes sense since they're doing the exact thing, but one takes a smarter approach and slows down to a faster orbit.
