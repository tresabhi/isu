# AERE 351 Homework 6

## 1.

![](https://i.imgur.com/reszTkC.png)

From the Internet:

$$
\mu = 3.98600 * 10^5 km^3/s^2
$$

$$
R = 6378km
$$

Given:

$$
h_1 = 300km
$$

$$
r_1 = h_1 + R = 6678km
$$

$$
e_1 = 0
$$

$$
r_{p2} = r_1 = 6678km
$$

$$
e_2 = 0.3
$$

$$
r_{a2} = r_{a3}
$$

$$
h_4 = 3000km
$$

$$
r_{p3} = r_4 = h_4 + R = 9378km
$$

Preliminaries:

$$
a_1 = \frac{r_{a1} + r_{p1}}{2} = r_1 = 6678km
$$

$$
p_1 = a_1 (1 - e_1^2) = a_1 = 6678km
$$

Velocities:

$$
v_1 = v_{\theta 1} = \sqrt{\frac{\mu}{p_1}} \cancel{(1 + e_1 \cos \theta)} = \sqrt{\frac{3.98600 * 10^5 km^3/s^2}{6678km}} = 7.726 km/s
$$

$$
e_2 = \frac{r_{a2} - r_{p2}}{r_{a2} + r_{p2}} = 0.3 = \frac{r_{a2} - 6678km}{r_{a2} + 6678km} \implies r_{a2} = 12402km
$$

$$
a = \frac{r_{a2} + r_{p2}}{2} = \frac{12402km + 6678km}{2} = 9540km
$$

$$
p_2 = a_2 (1 - e_2^2) = 9540km * (1 - 0.3^2) = 8681.4km
$$

$$
v_{p2} = \sqrt{\frac{\mu}{p_2}} (1 + e_2) = \sqrt{\frac{3.98600 * 10^5 km^3/s^2}{8681.4km}} (1 + 0.3) = 8.809 km/s
$$

$$
v_{a2} = \sqrt{\frac{\mu}{p_2}} (1 - e_2) = \sqrt{\frac{3.98600 * 10^5 km^3/s^2}{8681.4km}} (1 - 0.3) = 4.743 km/s
$$

$$
r_{a3} = r_{a2} = 12402km
$$

$$
e_3 = \frac{r_{a3} - r_{p3}}{r_{a3} + r_{p3}} = \frac{12402km - 9378km}{12402km + 9378km} = 0.1388
$$

$$
a_3 = \frac{r_{a3} + r_{p3}}{2} = \frac{12402km + 9378km}{2} = 10890km
$$

$$
p_3 = a_3 (1 - e_3^2) = 10890km * (1 - 0.1388^2) = 10680km
$$

$$
v_{a3} = \sqrt{\frac{\mu}{p_3}} (1 - e_3) = \sqrt{\frac{3.98600 * 10^5 km^3/s^2}{10680km}} (1 - 0.1388) = 5.26123 km/s
$$

$$
v_{p3} = \sqrt{\frac{\mu}{p_3}} (1 + e_3) = \sqrt{\frac{3.98600 * 10^5 km^3/s^2}{10680km}} (1 + 0.1388) = 6.957 km/s
$$

$$
p_4 = a_4 = r_4 = 9378km
$$

$$
v_4 = \sqrt{\frac{\mu}{p_4}} = \sqrt{\frac{3.98600 * 10^5 km^3/s^2}{9378km}} = 6.520 km/s
$$

$$
\Delta v_A = v_{p2} - v_1 = 8.809 km/s - 7.726 km/s = 1.083 km/s
$$

$$
\Delta v_B = v_{a3} - v_{a2} = 5.26123 km/s - 4.743 km/s = 0.5182 km/s
$$

$$
\Delta v_C = v_{p3} - v_4 = 6.957 km/s - 6.520 km/s = 0.437 km/s
$$

$$
\Delta v = \Delta v_A + \Delta v_B + \Delta v_C = 1.083 km/s + 0.5182 km/s + 0.437 km/s = \boxed{2.038 km/s}
$$
