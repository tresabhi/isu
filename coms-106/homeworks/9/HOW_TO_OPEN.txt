JavaJam Coffee Bar — Chapter 8 Solution
=======================================

HOW TO OPEN
1. Extract/unzip JavaJam_Chapter_8_Solution.zip.
2. Open the JavaJam_Chapter_8_Solution folder.
3. Double-click menu.html to view the completed Chapter 8 Menu page.
4. For the Home page, double-click index.html.

Do not move individual HTML files outside this folder, as they use relative paths
to JavaJam's stylesheet and images.

WHAT CHANGED FROM THE UPLOADED CHAPTER 7 STARTER
- javajam.css: Added table, td/th, and odd-row styles ABOVE @media blocks.
- menu.html: Replaced the three menu sections with a three-row, two-column table.
- Removed the now-unneeded #flow wrapper from menu.html so the table's
  90% width and centered margin behave normally at both screen widths.
- All other HTML, images, and the existing Chapter 7 stylesheet preserved.

The source starter's SOURCE_NOTES.md documents preexisting image substitutions
and a Jobs-page placeholder; those are carried over unchanged.
